About

Md Masuduzzaman

I work at the scale where the usual equations stop being reliable — inside channels a few molecules wide, where confinement, surface charge, and interfacial structure decide how fluid, ions, and heat actually move. My work joins molecular dynamics and continuum theory to describe that regime predictively.
