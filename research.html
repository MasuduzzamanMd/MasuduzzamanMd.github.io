Research focus

Molecular-scale transport phenomena in nanoconfined and electrochemical systems, integrating molecular dynamics simulations, machine learning potentials, and continuum modeling — ion and solvent transport under sub-nanometer confinement, electric double layer physics, electrokinetic processes, and nanoscale heat and mass transfer.

Research contributions

  • Electroosmotic flow and double layer structure — molecular simulation frameworks for electrokinetic transport in sub-1 nm nanochannels, uncovering how ion concentration, charge distribution, and confinement influence flow behavior and viscosity.
  • Nanodroplet wetting dynamics — interfacial mechanics of ionic nanodroplets from atomistic modeling, quantifying the impact of dissimilar molecular boundaries on contact angle and surface interaction.
  • Thermal transport at dissimilar interfaces — the validity and breakdown of Fourier's law at the nanoscale using non-equilibrium molecular dynamics, emphasizing atomic-scale heterogeneity in thermal resistance.
  • Continuum-to-atomistic transition modeling — multiscale models integrating statistical thermodynamics with classical continuum mechanics for transport under extreme confinement.
  • Strategic research development — conception and writing of research proposals funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea on nanoscale semiconductor processes, high-density fuel cells, and interfacial energy transport.
